How to read the score

Attention score ≠ investment score.

  1. 55 points: strongest fall relative to its candidate threshold.
  2. 25 points: breadth across 1-, 5- and 20-day windows.
  3. 10 points: confirmation across multiple candidate windows.
  4. 10 points: volume above the 20-day average.
